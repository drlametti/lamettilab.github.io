# Speaking

![AI Literacy](../assets/images/AI_literacy.jpeg)

*Halifax Nerd Nite, 2026 ([photo: CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/player/play/video/9.7135128))*

In addition to the lab's research on human-AI interaction, Dan gives accessible and engaging talks to the public and organizations about AI literacy—how language-based AI like ChatGPT works, and how to understand, evaluate, and effectively engage with AI systems in work and life.

Dan's free [Critical AI Literacy course](https://explore.acadiau.ca/product?catalog=AI-Literacy) has enrolled more than 7k people.
